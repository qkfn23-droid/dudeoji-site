What is a Character Counter?

Counts characters, words, sentences, lines, and bytes in your text in real time. Essential for social media posts, essays, SEO optimization, and any content with character limits.

Common Character Limits

  • Twitter/X — 280 characters
  • Instagram caption — 2,200 characters
  • Meta description (SEO) — 155-160 characters
  • YouTube title — 100 characters

FAQ

Are spaces counted as characters?

Yes, spaces are included in the character count by default. Character count with and without spaces are both displayed.
