Repayment Methods

Equal Installments: The combined principal and interest payment is the same each month. This is the most common repayment method.

Equal Principal: The principal payment is the same each month, while interest decreases as the balance goes down. Higher initial payments but less total interest.

Balloon Payment: Only interest is paid during the loan term, with the full principal repaid at maturity.
