What is FIRE?

FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) is a movement aimed at achieving financial independence and retiring early. The core principle is simple:

The 4% Rule: Save 25 times your annual expenses. Withdrawing 4% per year will not deplete your assets.
Example: Monthly expenses $3,000 → Annual $36,000 → Target = $36,000 × 25 = $900,000

The higher your savings rate, the faster you reach FIRE. A 50% savings rate means roughly 17 years; 70% means roughly 8.5 years.

FIRE variations: Lean FIRE (minimal expenses), Fat FIRE (comfortable lifestyle), Barista FIRE (part-time work + investment income).
