What is Korean Romanization?

The Revised Romanization of Korean is the official romanization system adopted by South Korea in 2000. It converts Korean (Hangul) characters into Roman letters. , , usedone.

Each Korean syllable is composed of an initial consonant, a vowel, and optionally a final consonant. The romanization converts each component into its corresponding Roman letter representation.
