What is Severance Pay? (Korean Labor Law)

Severance pay is a legally mandated payment under the Act on the Guarantee of Workers' Retirement Benefits in Korea. Employers must pay severance to any employee who has worked 1 year or more.

  • Eligibility: Continuous employment of 1+ year, working 15+ hours per week
  • Formula: Avg. daily wage (last 3 months) × 30 days × (days employed ÷ 365)
  • Avg. daily wage includes: base salary + 3/12 of annual bonus + 3/12 of unused leave pay
  • Payment deadline: Within 14 days of the last working day

This calculator provides an estimate for reference only. Actual severance pay may differ based on the terms of your employment contract.
