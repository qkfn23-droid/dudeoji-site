What is Average Cost?

Average cost (average purchase price) is the mean price per share paid when the same stock is purchased multiple times at different prices. The formula is Total Amount Spent ÷ Total Shares Bought.

When you buy more shares as the price falls (averaging down), your average cost decreases. When you buy more as the price rises (averaging up), your average cost increases. Knowing your average cost lets you quickly assess whether you are currently in profit or loss relative to the market price.
