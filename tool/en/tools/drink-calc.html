⚠️ Warning: This calculator provides an estimate based on the Widmark formula. Actual BAC may vary depending on individual factors (metabolism, food intake, liver function, etc.). Never drive after drinking. Legal DUI limit in South Korea: 0.03% BAC or above.

Widmark Formula

BAC = (Total alcohol consumed / (Body weight × Body water ratio)) − (Hours elapsed × Elimination rate)

Body water ratio: Male 0.68, Female 0.55
Elimination rate: ~0.015% per hour (average)
Volume per cup: Soju 50 ml, Beer 355 ml, Wine 150 ml, Makgeolli 300 ml, Whiskey 30 ml, Highball 250 ml

South Korea DUI Penalties:
• 0.03% – 0.08%: License suspension + fine
• 0.08% or above: License revocation + criminal charges
• 0.2% or above: Aggravated penalties
