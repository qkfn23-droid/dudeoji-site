What is Dividend Investing?

Dividend investing is a strategy that takes advantage of companies distributing a portion of their profits to shareholders as cash. It is ideal for investors who want a regular cash flow.

Dividend Yield = (Dividend per Share / Share Price) × 100
Example: Share price $50, dividend per share $2 → Dividend yield 4%

Reinvesting dividends creates a compounding effect that significantly grows your assets over the long term. Check the reinvestment effect in the simulation above.
