💡 How does it work?
First letters: First letter of each word + numbers + special characters
Mixed: Compress sentence with mixed case/numbers/specials
Leet: Replace letters with numbers/symbols (a→@, e→3, s→$)
"I was born in Seoul in 1995 and love pizza"
→ First letters: N2e$eSs!
→ Mixed: Na24$eUl#sG
→ Leet: n@2024$30uL!
🧠 Why are sentence passwords good?
• Easy to remember — Sentences are more memorable than random strings
• Long and complex — Extracting from sentences naturally creates length
• Only you know it — Personal sentences resist dictionary attacks
• Different per site — Add site name to sentence for unique passwords
