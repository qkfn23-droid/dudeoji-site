What is Four Pillars (Saju)?

Four Pillars of Destiny (Saju Palja) is a fate analysis system used in East Asia for over 1000 years. It analyzes life through four pillars (year, month, day, hour) and eight characters of Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. It interprets personality, aptitude, wealth, and relationships through the balance and interaction of the Five Elements (wood, fire, earth, metal, water).

FAQ

Is Saju different from astrology?

Saju is based on Yin-Yang Five Elements and Heavenly Stems/Earthly Branches, while astrology is based on planetary positions. Both use birth date but have different systems.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Basic analysis is possible with 3 pillars excluding the hour pillar. However, birth time is needed for more accurate analysis.

How old is the Four Pillars system?

The system was established during the Tang Dynasty (7th-10th century) and developed into its modern form during the Song Dynasty.
