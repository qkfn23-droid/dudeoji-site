What is Biorhythm?

Biorhythm is a theory that human physical, emotional, and intellectual states change in regular cycles. Physical (23 days), emotional (28 days), and intellectual (33 days) cycles help assess your current condition based on your birthdate.

How to Use

1. Enter your birthdate.
2. Your biorhythm cycles are displayed.
3. Check your physical, emotional, and intellectual states.
