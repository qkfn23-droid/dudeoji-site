The Life of a Star

(Everything here is my personal opinion. If you disagree, you are right.)

The most abundant thing in the universe

What is the most common thing in the universe?

Stars, they say.

Too many to count. Literally.

Where are stars born?

There is something called the "Pillars of Creation."

A massive cloud of gas floating in space.

Gas clumps together, and together, and together — until one moment it starts glowing on its own.

That is how a star is born, they say.

Now I understand why they call it the "Pillars of Creation."

What does a star burn?

Stars burn hydrogen to shine, they say.

Our sun is burning right now, at this very moment.

But it cannot burn forever.

How stars die

Stars die too, they say.

And there are 3 ways to die.

1. It quietly fades. As it cools, it scatters its gas shell into space. That gas joins a cloud again — clumps together and becomes a new star. A star dies so a star can be born.

2. It explodes and gets compressed to an extreme.

3. It explodes — and becomes something that not even light can escape.

Number 3 is a black hole.

The chilling part

Guess what is at the center of our galaxy.

A black hole.

That same black hole — born from a dead star.

Our sun and all the stars in our galaxy orbit around this black hole.

And it is not just us.

Nearly every galaxy has a black hole at its center, they say.

How many galaxies are there?

Too many to count.

Galaxies

We call our galaxy the "Milky Way."

But ours is not the only one.

Andromeda. Sombrero. Cartwheel. Magellanic.

But all these names — humans made them up.

Sombrero galaxy looks like a hat. Cartwheel galaxy looks like a wheel.

We named them by how they looked to us.

What if

What if there were beings in the Andromeda galaxy?

What would they call their own galaxy?

"Andromeda"? That is a name humans gave it.

To them, their galaxy would be "our galaxy."

They would probably name things by how they look to them.

Just like we called one "Sombrero" — they might look at the Milky Way and name it after something familiar to them...

Names

Every name in the universe was given by humans.

Constellations. Planet names. Galaxy names.

The same planet, called differently by different civilizations.

The universe has no names. We gave them all.

Fascinating. Truly fascinating.
