What is Zodiac Compatibility?

Zodiac compatibility analyzes the harmony between two people by comparing their sun signs. It interprets compatibility based on the elements (fire, earth, air, water) and modalities (cardinal, fixed, mutable) of the 12 zodiac signs.

How to Use

1. Select both people's zodiac signs.
2. Compatibility results are displayed.
3. Check the elemental and modality analysis.

FAQ

Are zodiac compatibility results accurate?

It's for fun and reference. Real relationships depend on effort and communication.

What elements are there?
