What is Hellenistic Astrology?

Hellenistic astrology developed in ancient Greece, combining Babylonian astronomy and Egyptian decan systems with Greek philosophy. It is the direct ancestor of modern Western astrology, introducing concepts like 12 houses and planetary dignities.
