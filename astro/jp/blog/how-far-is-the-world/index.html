世界はどこまでだろう？

（すべての内容は個人的な意見です。反論があればあなたが正しい 'ㅅ'）

The world

What is the world?

Work? School? News? Social media?

Or is it the country I live in? Or the entire planet?

The ancient world

For ancient people, the word for "world" was a bit different, they say.

In Sumer, they called it "An-Ki." Sky + Earth. That was the entire world.

In Greece, "Cosmos." The entire universe was the world.

In Chinese, "Tian Xia." Everything under the sky.

All of them included the sky.

Now

For us today, "world" = news, economy, AI, social media.

We removed the sky.

Besides "the clouds are pretty" or "the sky is clear" — something else.

Has humanity ever created anything from zero?

Airplanes = inspired by birds, they say.

Submarines = by fish.

Radar = by the ultrasound of bats.

Cameras = by the structure of the eye.

AI = by neural networks in the brain.

Everything was already in nature. We just referenced it, they say.

Not created from zero — observed and built.

Just like the ancients observed the sky and created astrology.

Then

Robots were made by observing humans.

Then what was the human made by observing?

Civilizations

Looking at history, there were civilizations.

Sumer. Egypt. Greece. Rome. Maya.

All were massive. All were brilliant.

All fell.

Causes

Sumer — the soil was ruined, they say. Too much farming.

Egypt — invaded from outside, divided from within.

Rome — rotted inside, collapsed outside.

Maya — drought came. The environment changed.

The causes are all different.

But there is one thing in common.

They all thought they would last forever.

Now

What about our civilization?

AI is here. Robots are here. We are reaching for space.

They say this is the most advanced era in history.

But past civilizations thought the same thing back then.

The Sumerians. The Romans. The Maya priests.

"We are the greatest."

But here another question arises.

Is what we know about ancient history really everything?

Take the Indus civilization, for example... we still cannot decipher their language.

And countless ancient clay tablets...

Can we really say we are the greatest when we do not fully know their stories?

The range of thought

What we think about every day.

Money. Work. School. Future. Retirement.

But the ancients looked at stars every day, thought about the universe, and pondered what comes after death.

Same humans, but different range of thought.

Why?

Just because the way of living is different?

How far

How far can humans think?

Looking at history — no world has lasted forever.

And this thing called thinking —

why do we do it?

Just because we are human...?

Fascinating. Truly fascinating.
